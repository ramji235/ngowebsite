OUR SERVICE AREAS

Access to justice starts with awareness.

Understanding rights and available options can be difficult, especially when people face legal or documentation challenges. Legal awareness and advisory support can help individuals make informed decisions and seek appropriate assistance.

Our legal awareness activities focus on promoting social justice and helping marginalized communities access guidance.

  • Build awareness of legal rights and processes
  • Provide information and guidance on common concerns
  • Encourage access to appropriate legal support
