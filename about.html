GET TO KNOW US

A Little Kindness
Can Create A Big Change.

Umeed means hope — and hope is at the heart of everything we aspire to do.

Umeed Foundation is a community-focused NGO concept dedicated to supporting people through education, food assistance and community development initiatives.

We believe meaningful progress happens when individuals, volunteers and communities work together. Our approach focuses on understanding local needs, encouraging participation and creating opportunities for a better tomorrow.
