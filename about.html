OUR INTRODUCTION

Building a healthy, empowered and self-reliant society.

Pragati Kalyan Samiti is a non-profit organization committed to inclusive social development across India. Since its inception, the organization has been actively working in healthcare, education, women empowerment, legal awareness, environmental sustainability and skill development, focusing on underprivileged communities.

We work to widen access to essential services and opportunities, support marginalized communities and encourage social justice, environmental responsibility and the preservation of Indian culture and traditional knowledge systems.
