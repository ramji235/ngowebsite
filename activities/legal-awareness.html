LEGAL AWARENESS · 2014–2026

Legal Awareness Programs

Making information about rights and access to justice easier to understand.

ABOUT THIS ACTIVITY

Knowing your rights is a step towards justice.

Pragati Kalyan Samiti lists legal awareness programs for 2014–2026. The activity focuses on helping community members learn about rights, responsibilities and where to seek appropriate help.

Clear information can help people make informed decisions and find qualified assistance when needed. General awareness is not a substitute for advice from a lawyer on an individual case.

  • Encourage awareness of legal rights and responsibilities
  • Share accessible information about justice and support pathways
  • Help people identify when to consult a qualified legal professional
