Women Empowerment Initiatives

Supporting greater participation, awareness and opportunity for women.

ABOUT THIS ACTIVITY

Stronger communities grow when women have opportunity.

Women empowerment initiatives are listed among Pragati Kalyan Samiti's activities for 2012–2026. The work reflects the organization's commitment to an inclusive society where women can participate, access information and pursue opportunities.

Empowerment is shaped by education, awareness, skills, safety and the ability to take part in decisions that affect everyday life.

  • Encourage women's participation in community life
  • Promote awareness, learning and confidence
  • Support equal access to opportunities and resources
