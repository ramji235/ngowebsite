Skill Development Programs

Supporting practical learning and pathways towards greater self-reliance.

ABOUT THIS ACTIVITY

Skills can open new opportunities.

Skill development programs are listed among Pragati Kalyan Samiti's activities since 2015. Practical learning can help participants build confidence, explore livelihood options and prepare for changing work opportunities.

The focus is on encouraging access to useful skills and supporting people as they work towards greater independence. Specific courses and availability may vary by program.

  • Promote practical, lifelong learning
  • Build confidence to explore livelihood opportunities
  • Encourage self-reliance through skills and knowledge
