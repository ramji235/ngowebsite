Promotion of Indian Culture

Recognizing the value of cultural heritage and traditional knowledge.

ABOUT THIS ACTIVITY

Heritage connects communities across generations.

Pragati Kalyan Samiti lists the promotion of Indian culture among its activities since 2011. This work reflects the organization's stated commitment to preserving Indian culture and traditional knowledge systems.

Cultural learning can create opportunities for communities to share traditions, understand their heritage and pass knowledge between generations while respecting India's diverse cultures.

  • Encourage appreciation of India's diverse cultural heritage
  • Recognize the value of traditional knowledge systems
  • Support intergenerational sharing and community learning
