HEALTHCARE · SINCE 2012

Monthly Health Check-up Camps

Encouraging awareness of health and access to community-based check-up support.

ABOUT THIS ACTIVITY

Health awareness begins with accessible information.

Pragati Kalyan Samiti lists monthly health check-up camps among its activities since 2012. The program focus is to make basic health awareness and check-up opportunities easier for community members to approach.

Health camps can also help people identify questions to discuss with qualified healthcare professionals. They do not replace a clinical diagnosis, treatment plan or emergency care.

  • Encourage people to pay attention to everyday health
  • Share basic health information in a community setting
  • Help participants understand when to seek professional medical advice
